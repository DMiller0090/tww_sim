# "The last cycle can be cut to leave the pusher ON the entry curve" (session 125, 2026-08-08)

> **status: historical** - this records an expectation that followed correctly from a sound measurement
> and that the next session's census refuted. What holds: the terminal predicate IS computable on a
> delivered pair, every herd endpoint that parks the pushed actor on the genuine side admits a clip
> roll, and the pusher ends the last roll 73-171 u from the nearest genuine entry. What was wrong is the
> inference that those 73-171 u were a RANKING deficit - that a last cycle cut against the entry curve
> would close them. They are a structural anti-correlation: carrying the pushed actor across the
> approach line and leaving the pusher back at the entry band are the same resource, and no roll does
> both. Current truth is
> [strategy/the-crossing-and-the-runway-are-one-resource.md](../strategy/the-crossing-and-the-runway-are-one-resource.md).

## What was claimed (session 125)

Having built the chain-back and measured that 15 of 127 banked endpoints park the pushed actor on the
genuine side and all 15 admit a clip roll:

> The real lever is upstream: the last cycle can now be searched against `entry_locus` directly, so it
> can be cut to leave the pusher on the curve instead of 74 u past it - which is where the frames below
> 94 are.

The reasoning was ordinary and, at the time, well supported. The endpoints on record all missed the
curve; the predicate that scores them had just become computable; the stage that produces them has a
junction with real authority over the pusher's position. Re-cutting it was the obvious next move.

## What it actually was (session 126)

Wiring the predicate in as the last cycle's endpoint rank took an afternoon and worked. What it ranked
did not. Over **20592 full-circle rolls** fired from three banked cycle-2 endpoints with every herd
prune off:

- **51 rolls** carry the pushed actor across the approach line - every one of them leaves the pusher at
  `runway` -48..89;
- **12366 rolls** leave the pusher at `runway` >= 190, where the entry curve is - the best crossing any
  of them achieves is **+80.4 u**, and that plateau is dead flat over six hundred units of runway
  because those rolls are not touching her at all;
- **zero rolls do both.**

The mechanism is not subtle in hindsight and it is the same one the roll stage already documented one
cycle down: carrying her sideways means rolling THROUGH her, and a roll that goes through her carries
the pusher just as far past the corner. The 73-171 u was never a deficit a better cut could recover -
it is what the last roll costs.

## The lesson

**A predicate becoming computable is not evidence that the stage it now scores can satisfy it.** The
s125 measurement said where the endpoints ARE; it said nothing about where that stage can PUT them, and
those are different questions answered by different sweeps. The cheap one - fire the full aim circle
with the herd's own prunes off and look at the joint distribution - costs a few minutes and would have
been available before the rank was wired. Ask what a stage can reach before ranking what it reached.
